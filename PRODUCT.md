## Users

- Retail buyers in Mexico who want authentic artisanal chiles curtidos and salsas, shipped to their door.
- Wholesale and export buyers (mayoreo, Guatemala and Central America) who need a separate path from retail.

Both audiences are served by the same site with distinct flows. How a sale is completed (checkout, WhatsApp, quote request) is not yet decided.

## Product Purpose

La Picosita de la Sierra, S.A. de C.V. is a Chiapas food company making traditional conservas: chiles curtidos and artisanal salsas, produced in Motozintla. The site presents the story and the catalog and turns visits into orders, retail and wholesale.

## Positioning

Born as a gastronomic-rescue research project by students and entrepreneurs of the Instituto Tecnológico de Frontera Comalapa (Unidad Motozintla). Chiles are roasted on a clay comal over oak wood, then pickled in fruit vinegar with no artificial additives. Chiles are bought directly from peasant families in Motozintla, Siltepec and Mazapa.

## Operating Context

Retail catalog with jar sizes. Chiles curtidos: 120g $30, 250g $40, 450g $55 MXN. Salsas: 270g $50, 350g $65, 450g $80 MXN. Shipping across Mexico, export to Guatemala. Expansion route: Motozintla, Frontera Comalapa, Guatemala and Central America. Spanish-language (es-MX).

## Product Principles

1. Rooted in place: Motozintla, the sierra, and the producing families are the proof, not generic "artisanal" claims.
2. Two buyers, two clear paths: retail ordering stays fast; wholesale and export get their own route.
3. Say only what is true: use confirmed facts and prices; never invent certifications, numbers, or reviews.
4. Heat is a product attribute: picor and flavor guide choice.
